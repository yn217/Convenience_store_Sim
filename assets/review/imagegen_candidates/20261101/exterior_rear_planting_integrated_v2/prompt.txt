Use case: game-asset-concept
Asset type: one continuous, isolated 2D exterior scenery sprite for a Japanese convenience-store management game; candidate for the rear-corner planting strip behind the store walls.
Primary request: a connected V-shaped planting landscape with two long arms meeting cleanly at a centered rear corner. Each arm is a shallow, straight isometric diagonal aligned to a precise 2:1 game grid slope (vertical change is half the horizontal run); the two arms form a symmetric rear-corner V. This must read as one continuous planter/landscape strip, not separate decorative tree icons.
Subject: dense Japanese spring landscaping with a few mature cherry trees, evergreen trees, low shrubs, small flowering plants, and restrained pale stone edging. Tree trunks and root bases extend to the lower silhouette so they can be naturally hidden behind store walls; canopies rise high enough to appear above the wall line. Keep the planting band narrow in depth and elongated along both diagonal arms.
Style/medium: polished painterly miniature isometric game art matching a premium Japanese convenience-store management game; crisp but soft illustrated edges, rich foliage layers, controlled detail, luminous daytime palette, clearly hand-painted rather than photorealistic.
Composition/framing: complete V shape fully visible, centered, generous transparent margin on every canvas edge; keep each arm straight and evenly long; show both tips and the apex with no cropping. Fixed isometric 2:1 diagonal slopes, no perspective lens, vertical trunks remain vertical. No store building, no wall, no road or parking, no foreground scene.
Lighting/mood: soft clear spring daylight, subtle contact shading within foliage only.
Background: true transparent alpha.
Constraints: one integrated rear planting group, no lettering or signage, no people, no pots, no detached planters, no buildings. Keep foliage low-alpha edges clean and keep the full silhouette well inside the canvas. The game will layer store walls in front to occlude lower trunks.
Avoid: shallow near-horizontal arms, wrong diagonal slope, photographic cutouts, green color fringe, alpha halos, edge-touching branches, tile seams, extra scenery, watermark, collage.
